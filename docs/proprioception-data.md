# Human data for a proprioception benchmark

The strongest first candidate is knee position reproduction from Nakashima et al. (2021). It reports errors in degrees and provides participant data under CC BY. Passive arm position matching from Contu et al. (2017) provides a complementary task with trial data. Both can support an external evaluation if the task model and analysis are specified before fitting. This note records a source and file audit on 27 October 2026. No benchmark, parameter fit or claim of human validity has been added.

## What the current module predicts

`SpindleProprioception` computes muscle spindle rates from muscle tendon length and velocity, adds independent Gaussian error to hinge joint angles and returns buffered signals after a fixed delay. Slide joints currently receive no position noise. Muscle forces in the returned sample are copied actuator forces. The module has no perceptual force or effort estimate, movement detection decision, position matching controller or memory for a target. Its joint angle estimate is generated independently of its spindle rates. The existing spindle test checks the implemented equation and signal buffering.

The spindle coefficients were fitted to cat hamstrings. Their equation agreement is a physiological consistency check, and does not validate human position matching. [Prochazka and Gorassini, 1998](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1469-7793.1998.277bu.x). The current joint noise parameter is used as a Gaussian standard deviation, while its cited 3° value describes absolute error in a bimanual elbow task. The source also reports 1.97° variable error for young participants in its initial rigid support condition. These quantities need an explicit task observation model before they can identify a sensory noise parameter. A comparison with the same elbow source would retain the consistency label. [Talis and Levik, 2016](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2016.00520).

For a zero mean Gaussian estimate with standard deviation `sigma`, expected absolute error is `sigma * sqrt(2/pi)`. A difference between two independent estimates with that same standard deviation has expected absolute error `2 * sigma / sqrt(pi)`. Matching also introduces memory, response and possibly motor error. Noise is currently redrawn on every `sense` call, so averaging successive samples changes the result with sampling frequency. A benchmark must declare how observations become one perceptual judgement. The fixed delay is sourced from a stretch response and cannot by itself specify a conscious movement detection threshold. The noise and delay provenance labels now record these mappings as assumed. Their numerical defaults remain unchanged. [Pruszynski et al., 2009](https://doi.org/10.1152/jn.00085.2009).

## First choice: knee position reproduction

[Nakashima et al. (2021), DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0256561](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0256561) tested 11 healthy adults, mean age 27.6 years. Participants were blindfolded. The nondominant knee was passively placed 30° or 60° from its relaxed starting position for 5 s, returned, then actively reproduced and held for 5 s. Three repetitions formed each participant's mean absolute angular error. Published VICON results are 3.16° with SD 1.10° at 30° and 2.62° with SD 1.39° at 60°. The simultaneous iPhone measurements concern the same participants and trials. The instrument comparison shares its human sample.

The [S1 workbook](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article/file?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0256561.s001&type=supplementary) was downloaded and inspected. Its 10,587 bytes have MD5 `1913a051b520372a72ab1c56fb287512`. It contains 11 participant means for each device and angle, without individual trials or trajectories. Recomputed means and SDs agree with Table 1. The article and supplement use CC BY; retain author attribution and the dataset DOI. Use the VICON columns for the primary outcome. The table's reliability SEM column should not be substituted for the sampling standard error of a participant mean. [Primary article and supporting information](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0256561).

This is the most direct mapping to the existing hinge joint output. It requires a passive presentation and active reproduction task, a stated target memory and stopping rule, and an account of motor and measurement error. Evaluate reproduction error in degrees. Relating sensory standard deviation to mean absolute reproduction error requires the task observation model. Keep the angles relative to the protocol's relaxed reference. The phone attachment supplied additional skin cues, and the sample was small. This study compares measurement methods and supplies no independent session reliability estimate. A task implementation should preserve the three repetitions and participant aggregation, with any acceptance interval and its interpretation declared in advance.

## Second choice: passive planar arm matching

[Contu et al. (2017), DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0183257](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0183257) studied nine healthy controls, mean age 54.8 years, and nine stroke participants. The robot presented a target 10 cm away at 7 cm/s, held it for 2 s, returned, then moved at 2 cm/s until the participant reported a match. Three directions were tested with vision occluded. Controls completed ten trials per direction. Published control absolute error is 1.5 cm with reported SEM 0.1 cm. Signed errors, absolute errors and within participant variability are distinct outcomes. Positive signed error denotes undershoot. A verbal report and investigator button press contribute to the stopping position, while passive movement reduces active movement demands.

The [CC BY 4.0 data record](https://api.figshare.com/v2/articles/5621770) lists separate healthy and stroke workbooks. The [healthy S1 file](https://ndownloader.figshare.com/files/9792826) was downloaded and inspected: 13,121 bytes, MD5 `25077ca061d478658c037ae6efe6fc00`, 30 trial rows and nine participant columns. It explicitly labels signed errors in centimetres. The corresponding [stroke S2 file](https://ndownloader.figshare.com/files/9792832) is separate. An analysis should reproduce the article's per participant, per direction medians and signed error standard deviations, then retain participant clustering when calculating uncertainty. Published group summaries and a new pooled trial analysis can have different uncertainty estimates.

This task needs arm geometry, a transformation from joint estimates to hand position, memory for the first position and a report and stopping pathway. The released workbook contains errors. Joint trajectories and individual arm geometry needed to identify those mechanisms are absent. It can evaluate a complete declared task model; it cannot isolate spindle gain or joint noise without additional assumptions. Use the healthy controls for an initial baseline evaluation. Modeling stroke would require separately justified impairment mechanisms.

## Human spindle recordings

[Torell and Dimitriou (2024), DOI 10.1016/j.celrep.2024.114699](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2024.114699) recorded 14 muscle spindle afferents in eight healthy humans during local forearm pressure and passive wrist movement. The [Mendeley data, version 2, DOI 10.17632/549wt8drc7.2](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/549wt8drc7/2) are labelled CC BY 4.0. The record describes normalized firing changes, EMG, angle and velocity summaries, ensemble time series averaged over 100 ms and regression coefficients. These are genuine human spindle observations, distinct from the cat fits used by the package. The [published paper](https://umu.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2%3A1904012/FULLTEXT01) distinguishes public analyzed datasets from original recordings available on request.

The landing page, licence and described sheet contents were verified. File downloads returned HTTP 403 in this audit, so the workbook and its checksum remain unverified. Normalized summaries cannot directly validate absolute impulses per second or the baseline firing parameter. The current length and velocity equation also has no local pressure or pressure history input. A physiological evaluation would need compatible normalization, appropriate muscle mechanics and the actual stimulus traces. Adding surface contact force directly to spindle firing would introduce an unvalidated mapping.

## Newer data and access limits

[Ünver et al. (2026), DOI 10.1152/jn.00476.2025](https://doi.org/10.1152/jn.00476.2025) studied 60 healthy adults aged 18 to 30 using active movement extent discrimination and targeted pinch force grading. [Zenodo record 19101754](https://zenodo.org/records/19101754), DOI `10.5281/zenodo.19101754`, is open under CC BY 4.0. Its [AMEDA workbook](https://zenodo.org/api/records/19101754/files/AMEDA%20AUC%20hesap.xlsx/content) was downloaded: 177,224 bytes, MD5 `c38e493ddd23e892dfc455815bb0492a`. It contains categorical responses and AUC calculations. The accompanying 135 MB RAR was verified by metadata and an HTTP range request, but its contents were not inspected. This is a useful later discrimination and force perception extension. A categorical decision model, verified stimulus values and a force perception pathway are required before scoring it; the current module supplies none of those outcomes.

[Mortazavi Najafabadi et al. (2025), DOI 10.1016/j.bbe.2025.08.005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbe.2025.08.005), offers ankle motion and position outcomes with interday reliability measurements. Its [RepOD dataset](https://doi.org/10.18150/YY9SOD) is CC0. The [original workbook](https://repod.icm.edu.pl/api/access/datafile/77792?format=original) contains six sheets covering young and older groups, motion, passive position and active position. Its 34,197 bytes match MD5 `d510eea1e4822187d3112ab54a5c2060`. The preview exposes only the first sheet. An apparent formula inconsistency needs clarification before use: in `Old - Passive SOP`, cells `K2` and `Q2` subtract 6° although their headings specify targets of 10° and 14°. Young and older sheets also appear to encode trial values differently. Do not silently correct these values or adopt cached averages as reference outcomes. The complete protocol, target definitions and intended preprocessing need confirmation.

The [2026 KU Leuven arm matching release](https://doi.org/10.48804/KGMBIX) provides hand kinematics in metres from four studies, but specifies CC BY NC SA 4.0. It therefore has a noncommercial condition. Its metadata and file listing were checked; its large archives were not downloaded. The [2016 wrist matching dataset](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/vncp79b6sf/1), DOI `10.17632/vncp79b6sf.1`, specifies GPLv3 despite its associated article being CC BY. Neither release should be described as unrestricted data suitable for bundling with the package's current licences.

## Evaluation plan

Begin with the knee task and the published VICON aggregates. Keep current parameters fixed for an initial external comparison and label task settings as assumed. Declare one primary outcome, participant aggregation and acceptance policy before evaluating predictions. If a task or noise parameter is fitted to these data, report its fitting outcomes as calibration and reserve a different dataset or a genuinely unused condition for evaluation. An unused angle from the same participants remains a shared participant evaluation. Use the passive arm data as a later task generalization test after specifying its geometry and stopping pathway.

Record download URLs, exact versions, file checksums, licences, units and extraction procedures alongside any derived reference table. The workbook checksums above were computed locally from downloaded bytes using Python's `hashlib`; spreadsheet contents were inspected through `zipfile` and XML. The healthy arm, original ankle and AMEDA workbook hashes match the respective repository metadata. The knee hash is a locally computed file identity. Files were inspected in memory, and no participant records were added to this repository. Keep participant data outside `bench/references.py`; only the cited aggregate and its declared acceptance range belong there. Estimate uncertainty at the participant level for these repeated measurement designs. Simulation seed variation and human sampling uncertainty should remain separate. The present module cannot support a standalone claim about conscious movement detection, human spindle firing, effort perception or clinical impairment merely by adding another numerical score.
